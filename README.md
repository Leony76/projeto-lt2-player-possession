# 🪓​ Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession

## 📖​ About 

Welcome to Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession, a platform created to help Lumber Tycoon 2 players organize, manage, and audit their in-game item collections. Whether you own rare limited items or conventional items, this system allows you to keep track of your possessions, monitor their quantities and market values, and explore the collections of other registered players.

Our goal is to provide a convenient and organized way for players to document their inventories, keep track of their collections, and have a better understanding of the items they own and their potential value within the Lumber Tycoon 2 community.

## 🏚️ Home

The Home page provides a quick overview of your account and your collection. You can see your registered contacts and access their profiles, as well as preview some of the items in your personal inventory. It also displays the total number of contacts and registered items, making it easier to keep track of your account activity and quickly navigate to the sections you use most.

## 📦 Items

The Items section contains the platform's item catalog, where you can explore the items registered in the system. You can search for specific items by name and narrow down the results using filters such as category, state, and advanced filtering options.

Each item displays relevant information, including its image, name, category, condition, original price, and average market price when available. This section helps players identify items, compare their information, and better understand their place within the game's trading community.

Both limited and conventional items can be documented, making the catalog useful for players with different types of collections.

## 🧔📦 My Items

The My Items section is your personal inventory manager. Here, you can register the items you own, specify their quantities, and organize your collection in one place.

You can add new items, update their information, adjust quantities, and remove items from your inventory whenever necessary. Search and filtering options make it easier to find specific entries, even when your collection contains many different items.

Each registered item displays its category, state, image, quantity, and available price information. This allows you to maintain an organized record of your possessions and estimate the overall size and potential value of your collection.

Whether you own a few common items or an extensive collection of rare and limited items, this section helps you keep everything documented.

## 📞 Contacts

The Contacts section allows you to discover and explore other players registered on Lumber Tycoon Player's Possession. You can search for users by their usernames and use advanced filters to find specific players more easily.

Each player card provides a preview of their profile, including their username, profile picture, registration date, and the number of items they have registered. Depending on your relationship with that player, you can also see whether you are already connected, whether a contact request is pending, or whether you can send a new contact request.

This section makes it easier to discover other collectors and explore the collections they have chosen to document on the platform.

## 💬 Conversation System

The Conversation System allows players who have established a contact relationship to communicate with each other through private, real-time conversations.

Players can access their conversations, exchange messages with their contacts, and keep track of their communication history in one place. Messages are delivered in real time, allowing both participants to interact without needing to refresh the page.

The system also provides features such as unread message indicators, message history, and conversation management, making it easier to keep track of ongoing discussions and stay connected with other collectors.

Each conversation is associated with its participants, ensuring that only authorized users can access and exchange messages within it.

This feature adds a social dimension to Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession, allowing collectors to communicate, discuss their collections, share information about items, and interact more easily with their contacts.

## 🧔📞 My Contacts

The My Contacts section brings together the players with whom you have established or pending contact relationships.

You can view your contacts, check their profile information, see how many items they have registered, and identify the current relationship status between you and each player.

You can also search your contact list, filter users by relationship status, and manage your existing connections. This provides a convenient way to keep track of players you know and quickly access their collections.

## 🧔 Profile

Every registered player has a profile containing information about their account and their documented collection.

Your own profile allows you to view your public account information, such as your username, account identifier, Lumber Bucks balance, profile picture, and other available account details. You can also update your profile picture by providing an image URL or uploading an image.

When viewing another player's profile, you can explore the information they have made available, including their username, account identifier, Lumber Bucks balance, number of contacts, total registered items, and registration date.

You can also see their contact status and, when applicable, manage your relationship with that player. Below the profile information, you can browse their registered contacts and item collection.

This makes player profiles an important part of the platform, allowing collectors to showcase their documented possessions and giving other players a better understanding of their collections.

## 🚪​🔒 Account Registration and Sign In

To use Lumber Tycoon Player's Possession, you need to create an account or sign in with an existing one. The registration process requires a username and password, while account creation also includes password confirmation.

Once authenticated, you can access your personal inventory, manage contacts, explore other players' profiles, and use the platform's features.

Your account provides a centralized place to maintain your collection records and interact with other registered players.

## 🌟💡​🌟​ Our Purpose

Lumber Tycoon Player's Possession was created to bring more organization and transparency to the Lumber Tycoon 2 collecting community.

In a game where players can own a wide variety of items, including rare limited collectibles and conventional items, keeping track of everything can become difficult. This platform aims to make that process easier by providing a centralized inventory system where players can register their possessions, record quantities, consult item information, and explore other players' documented collections.

It is important to remember that Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession is an independent inventory and collection management platform. The information displayed depends on what each player registers and maintains in the system. Item prices, including average market prices, are references for collection auditing and should not be interpreted as guaranteed trading values or official prices established by the game.

Our mission is simple: help Lumber Tycoon 2 players organize their collections, keep track of their possessions, discover other collectors, and make their inventory information easier to access and understand.

Thank you for being part of Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession!

# 🛠️ Technologies Used

Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession was developed using a combination of modern technologies to provide a responsive user experience, a structured backend architecture, secure authentication, database management, and real-time communication.

- **Node.js** – JavaScript runtime used to execute the backend application.
- **TypeScript** – Provides static typing and better code organization.
- **Express 5** – Web framework responsible for handling HTTP requests, routes, and middleware.
- **Prisma ORM** – Database toolkit used to interact with PostgreSQL and manage database migrations.
- **PostgreSQL** – Relational database used to store application data.
- **Socket.IO** – Enables real-time communication and messaging features.
- **JSON Web Token (JWT)** – Handles authentication and token-based session management.
- **Argon2** – Used for secure password hashing.
- **Cloudinary** – Handles image storage and management.
- **Multer** – Processes file uploads.
- **Cookie Parser** – Parses cookies received in HTTP requests.
- **CORS** – Controls cross-origin resource sharing.
- **Express Rate Limit** – Helps protect endpoints against excessive requests.
- **Zod** – Provides schema validation and data validation.

### Testing and Code Quality

- **Vitest** – Testing framework used to write and execute automated tests.
- **Oxlint** – Static code analysis tool used to identify potential issues and maintain code quality.

### Development and Deployment

- **Git and GitHub** – Version control and source code hosting.
- **Docker** – Containerization platform used to package and run the application in consistent environments.
- **Docker Compose** – Orchestrates the frontend, backend, and PostgreSQL containers.

# 🚀 Getting Started

This section explains how to download, configure, and run Lumber Tycoon 2 Player's Possession on your local machine.

You can run the project either directly on your computer or using Docker.

## 📋 Prerequisites

Before getting started, make sure you have the following tools installed:

- **Git** – To clone the repository.
- **Node.js 24 or later** – Required to run the frontend and backend.
- **npm** – Package manager included with Node.js.
- **PostgreSQL** – Required when running the project without Docker.

Alternatively, you can use Docker to run the entire application without installing Node.js or PostgreSQL directly on your machine.

- **Docker Desktop** – Recommended for Windows and macOS users.
- **Docker Compose** – Included with Docker Desktop.

## 📥 1. Clone the Repository

Open a terminal and clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Leony76/projeto-lt2-player-possession
```

Navigate to the project directory:

```bash
cd projeto-lt2-player-possession
```

## ⚙️ 2. Configure Environment Variables

The project requires environment variables to configure the database connection and authentication secrets.

Create a `.env` file in the project root using `.env.example` as a reference.

For example:

```bash
cp .env.example .env
```

On Windows PowerShell, you can also use:

```powershell
Copy-Item .env.example .env
```

Open the newly created `.env` file and configure the required values.

Example:

```dotenv
POSTGRES_USER=postgres
POSTGRES_PASSWORD=change_me
POSTGRES_DB=lumber_tycoon

DATABASE_URL=postgresql://postgres:change_me@db:5432/lumber_tycoon?schema=public

JWT_SECRET=your_secure_jwt_secret
JWT_REFRESH_SECRET=your_secure_refresh_secret
```

Make sure to replace the example values with your own credentials and secrets.

**Important:** Never share or commit your `.env` file. It contains private configuration values.

# 💻 Running Locally (Without Docker)

If you prefer to run the frontend and backend directly on your computer, follow these steps.

### 1. Install Dependencies

Install the dependencies from the project root:

```bash
npm install
```

Then install the backend dependencies:

```bash
cd backend
npm install
```

Return to the project root and install the frontend dependencies:

```bash
cd ..
cd frontend
npm install
```

Alternatively, if you want to reproduce the exact dependency versions recorded in the lockfiles, use `npm ci` in each directory instead of `npm install`.

### 2. Configure PostgreSQL

Make sure PostgreSQL is installed and running on your machine.

Create a database for the project and configure the backend's `DATABASE_URL` to connect to it.

For a local PostgreSQL installation, the connection string should use `localhost`:

```dotenv
DATABASE_URL=postgresql://postgres:your_password@localhost:5432/lumber_tycoon?schema=public
```

Make sure the database name, username, password, and port match your local PostgreSQL configuration.

When running the backend directly on your computer, ensure that the required environment variables are available to the backend process. The Prisma configuration loads environment variables through `dotenv/config`; if commands are executed from the `backend` directory, provide the appropriate `.env` file there or configure the variables in your terminal.

### 3. Generate the Prisma Client

Navigate to the backend directory:

```bash
cd backend
```

Generate the Prisma Client:

```bash
npx prisma generate
```

### 4. Apply Database Migrations

Run the following command to apply the existing migrations:

```bash
npx prisma migrate deploy
```

### 5. Populate the Database

Run the seed command to populate the database with the initial data:

```bash
npx prisma db seed
```

### 6. Start the Backend

Start the backend development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The backend will run on:

```text
http://localhost:3000
```

Keep this terminal running.

### 7. Start the Frontend

Open another terminal and navigate to the frontend directory:

```bash
cd frontend
```

Start the frontend development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The frontend will be available at:

```text
http://localhost:5173
```

You can now access the application through your browser.

# 🐳 Running with Docker

Docker provides an alternative way to run the entire application without manually installing Node.js, PostgreSQL, or configuring each service individually.

The project includes Dockerfiles for the frontend and backend, along with a Docker Compose configuration to orchestrate the application containers and database.

### 1. Install Docker

Download and install Docker Desktop:

https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/

After installation, make sure Docker Desktop is running.

### 2. Configure Environment Variables

Make sure you have created and configured the `.env` file in the project root, following the instructions in the environment configuration section.

For Docker, the database connection string must use the PostgreSQL service name instead of `localhost`:

```dotenv
DATABASE_URL=postgresql://postgres:your_password@db:5432/lumber_tycoon?schema=public
```

This is because Docker Compose services communicate with each other through their service names.

### 3. Build and Start the Containers

From the project root, execute:

```bash
docker compose up -d --build
```

This command builds the necessary images and starts the containers in detached mode.

The Docker Compose configuration handles the startup order and database initialization, including:

- Starting the PostgreSQL database.
- Waiting for the database to become healthy.
- Generating the Prisma Client.
- Applying pending database migrations.
- Executing the database seed.
- Starting the backend and frontend services.

### 4. Access the Application

Once all services have started successfully, access the application through:

**Frontend:**
```text
http://localhost:5173
```

**Backend:**
```text
http://localhost:3000
```

### 5. Useful Docker Commands

Here are some useful commands for managing the application:

**Start the containers:**

```bash
docker compose up -d
```

**Rebuild images and start the containers:**

```bash
docker compose up -d --build
```

**Stop and remove the containers:**

```bash
docker compose down
```

**View running containers:**

```bash
docker compose ps
```

**View application logs:**

```bash
docker compose logs -f
```

**View logs from a specific service:**

```bash
docker compose logs -f backend
```

```bash
docker compose logs -f frontend
```

**Restart the services:**

```bash
docker compose restart
```

**Remove containers, networks, and database volumes:**

```bash
docker compose down -v
```

**Warning:** The last command permanently deletes the PostgreSQL data stored in the Docker volume. Only use it if you intentionally want to reset the database.

- The application requires a working database connection to operate correctly.
